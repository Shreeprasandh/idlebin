Statutory Rules & Legal Penalties Reference

Solid Waste Management Rules 2016 (MoEFCC) & Greater Chennai Corporation Sanitation Bye-Laws 2019

STATUTORY LAW
SWM Rules 2016: Rule 4(1)(a)
Every waste generator shall segregate and store waste generated by them in three separate streams: biodegradable, non-biodegradable, and domestic hazardous waste in suitable colored bins before handover.
GCC Bye-Laws 2019: Schedule II Fines
Spot fine of Rs. 250 for 1st residential non-segregation default; Rs. 500 for repeat defaults. Commercial non-compacted volume dumpers are levied Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 5,000 under the Municipal Municipal Corporation Act.
Staff Conservancy Bounty: 10% Credit
To incentivize civic accountability and prevent street bribery, 10% of each statutory penalty collected is automatically credited to the conservancy staff welfare fund upon timestamped photo verification.
