1. WHAT IS IDLEBIN IN ONE SENTENCE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IdleBin is a zero-electricity, foot-operated dustbin that mechanically crushes 
plastic bottles and packets flat as you step on it, holding 4 times more waste 
so garbage never spills onto the street.


2. THE REAL PROBLEM ON INDIAN STREETS (THE "WHY")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Look at any street dustbin in India: It gets full in just 2 to 3 hours.
- Why? Not because people throw heavy trash, but because 80% OF WHAT IS INSIDE 
  IS JUST TRAPPED EMPTY AIR inside uncrushed plastic water bottles, disposable 
  tea cups, and snack packets.
- A normal 60L bin looks completely full after just 20 to 25 empty bottles!
- The moment the bin looks full:
  1. The next 50 people throw their garbage bags ON THE ROAD around the bin 
     (creating dirty "Kachra Points").
  2. People refuse to touch dirty bin lids, so they throw trash from 10 feet away.
  3. Stray cows and dogs rip the bags open, scattering rotten food and plastic 
     across the street.
  4. Rain washes that plastic into open drains (naalis), choking the drainage.


3. HOW IDLEBIN ACTUALLY WORKS (THE "HOW")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IdleBin is 100% mechanical. Zero electricity, zero batteries, zero sensors.

Step-by-Step Flow:
1. FOOT PEDAL: You walk up with your trash and step down on a pedal at the base.
2. HANDS-FREE: The lid opens automatically so nobody touches a dirty bin.
3. THE CRUSH STROKE: Stepping on the pedal pulls down a vertical rod connected 
   to an overhead lever arm. This forces a flat, heavy pressing plate (platen) 
   downward inside the bin like a piston.
4. 4X COMPACTION: Using your normal body weight (~40-50 kg), the plate crushes 
   bulky plastic bottles and snack packets flat with ~600 Newtons of force.
5. SPRING RESET: When you lift your foot, a heavy-duty tension spring pulls the 
   crushing plate back up to the top, ready for the next person.

Result: The exact same bin now holds 100+ bottles instead of 25! It stops 
overflowing between municipal collection days.


4. THE 2 BIG TOUGH QUESTIONS (AND HOW WE ANSWER THEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUESTION 1: "Won't the crushed plastic bottles bounce back up?"
OUR ANSWER:
- In real dustbins, trash doesn't sit alone. Once bottle #1 is crushed flat, 
  bottle #2 and wrapper #3 fall on top. The combined weight of the pile keeps 
  the lower layers held down.
- Inside the bin walls, we install simple downward-pointing rubber/plastic teeth 
  (retention baffles). When the plate pushes a bottle down, the teeth let it pass. 
  When the plate lifts back up, the teeth catch the bottle from the top and lock 
  it down.
- A 50 kg foot press permanently creases (deforms) the thin plastic walls of PET 
  bottles, so they lose 70% of their springiness.

QUESTION 2: "What about wet food waste? If crushed, it will leak smelly liquid!"
OUR ANSWER:
- In India's Swachh Bharat rules, dustbins are always paired: 
  GREEN = Wet Food Waste | BLUE = Dry Recyclables (Plastic, Cans, Paper).
- IDLEBIN IS STRICTLY FOR THE BLUE (DRY) BIN! 
  We DO NOT crush wet food. Wet waste is already dense food scraps with no trapped 
  air. It is the dry plastic bottles in the Blue Bin that cause 90% of street overflow.
- What if someone accidentally throws chai or wet food inside?
  IdleBin has a perforated false floor (drainage holes) at the bottom. Any stray 
  liquid drips into a sealed bottom tray filled with dry sawdust and activated 
  coconut charcoal. The charcoal kills the odor and absorbs moisture naturally.

9. THE SWM 2016 SEGREGATION ENFORCEMENT BREAKTHROUGH (NEW!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- THE INDIAN LAW: Rule 4 of the Solid Waste Management Rules 2016 makes it 
  mandatory for every citizen/shop to segregate waste into Wet, Dry, and Domestic 
  Hazardous waste. Municipalities have statutory powers to levy fines (Rs. 100 to 
  Rs. 1,000).
- WHY IT FAILS TODAY: Sanitation workers (Safai Karamcharis) face angry arguments 
  if they question residents. They have no formal reporting tool, and ward officers 
  lack photographic proof.
- OUR SOLUTION (THE SEGREGATION SENTINEL):
  1. Sanitation workers carry a 1-click photo-logging mobile interface.
  2. If a household drops unsegregated wet/chemical waste into the dry bin, 
     the worker flags the door/property ID with a quick photo.
  3. The Ward Sanitation Officer verifies the geo-tagged image in 2 taps.
  4. An automated municipal statutory notice (challan) is issued (Rs. 250 for 
     first offense, Rs. 500 for repeat) integrated into the property tax bill.
  5. WORKER INCENTIVE: A 10% civic vigilance credit (Rs. 25) is credited to 
     the worker's welfare fund for every verified report, transforming workers 
     from manual scavengers into empowered municipal compliance officers!


10. VEHICLE-MOUNTED MOBILE FILL SENSING & DYNAMIC REROUTING (NEW!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WHY FIXED SENSORS ARE NOT ENOUGH: Installing IoT ultrasonic sensors on 5,000 
  bins across a city is expensive and prone to vandalism or street battery theft.
- OUR VEHICLE-MOUNTED SOLUTION:
  1. Collection vehicles (auto-tippers / garbage trucks) have an on-board, 
     forward/side optical or LiDAR scanner mounted on the cabin.
  2. As the vehicle drives along main roads (Mount-Poonamallee), it scans 
     roadside dustbins from 15-20 meters away.
  3. ON-THE-FLY DYNAMIC REROUTING: If Bin #5 (Manapakkam) is scanned at only 
     42% fill, the vehicle's navigation system immediately bypasses that 
     narrow street in real-time, redirecting the driver directly to Bin #2 
     (DLF Cybercity) which is 91% full.
  4. IMPACT: Cuts truck travel from 16.8 km to 8.9 km (47% fuel reduction), 
     saving over Rs. 32,000 per month per cluster in municipal diesel!
